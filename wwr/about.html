Splash White Water Rafting

Splash White Water Rafting is proud to be an equal opportunity provider, offering guided experiences on a nondiscriminatory basis. SRE is licensed by the State of Idaho, fully bonded and insured, and holds permits issued by the Cottonwood, Idaho District of the Bureau of the Land Management and by the United States Forest Service, Wallowa-Whitman National Forest. Our team is committed to delivering safe, memorable adventures for guests of all skill levels, from first-time rafters to seasoned outdoor enthusiasts. We prioritize environmental stewardship, following strict guidelines to preserve the natural beauty of the rivers and surroundings.

History

Splash White Water Rafting began in 1978 with a vision to deliver high-quality and affordable whitewater experiences while respecting the environment.

Today, the company serves hundreds of guests daily with modern equipment, expert guides, and a passion for adventure and safety.
